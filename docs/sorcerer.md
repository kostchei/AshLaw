1. Anti-Magic & Countering

Sorcery is designed to shut down other wizards' spellcasting:



Spell Lock / Disjunction: Stripping enemy enchantments off units or node sites.

Counter Magic / Spell Blast: Interrupting enemy spells mid-cast on the overland map or in tactical combat.

Magic Immunity: Buffing units so they ignore enemy magical attacks entirely.

2. Illusions & Confusion

Sorcery utilizes real-versus-unreal mechanics:



Phantom Warriors / Phantom Beasts: Summoned units that hit hard, but if an enemy unit passes an Resistance check, the phantom damage vanishes.

Confusion / Mind Storm: Turning enemy armies against themselves or wrecking their combat stats.

3. Bypassing Physical Rules

Rather than blasting through a mountain like a Chaos wizard, a Sorcery wizard alters the rules of movement and interaction:



Flight / Wind Walking: Moving armies freely across terrain.

Invisibility: Bypassing enemy defenses entirely.

Node Mastery: Sorcery wizards can meld with Sorcery Nodes to gain massive power advantages over rival wizards trying to channel raw mana.
