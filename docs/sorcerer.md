# Sorcerer

The sorcerer is a Charisma-based defiler with metamagic and spellfire. Sorcerers
can draw life from creatures and terrain in a radius, or absorb power from
directly incoming spells.

## Base spell lists

- **Flesh Destruction:** rot, wither, and rupture living matter.
- **Solid Destruction:** disintegrate, shatter, or erode unliving objects and
  structures.
- **Gas/Fluid Destruction:** manipulate and vaporize non-solid elements.
- **Soul Destruction:** sever the soul from the body or annihilate it utterly.

## Anti-magic and countering

Sorcery is designed to shut down other spellcasters:

- **Spell Lock / Disjunction:** strip enchantments from units or node sites.
- **Counter Magic / Spell Blast:** interrupt spells during tactical or
  overland casting.
- **Magic Immunity:** ward units against hostile magical attacks.

## Illusions and confusion

Sorcery uses real-versus-unreal mechanics:

- **Phantom Warriors / Phantom Beasts:** summoned forces whose damage vanishes
  when the target passes a Resistance check.
- **Confusion / Mind Storm:** turn enemies against one another or wreck their
  combat effectiveness.

## Bypassing physical rules

Rather than breaking physical obstacles through raw force, a sorcerer changes
the rules of interaction:

- **Flight / Wind Walking:** move freely across terrain.
- **Invisibility:** bypass mundane defenses.
- **Node Mastery:** meld with Sorcery Nodes to overpower rival attempts to
  channel raw mana.
